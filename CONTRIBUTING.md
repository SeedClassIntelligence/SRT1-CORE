# Contributing to SRT-1 Core

We love your input! We want to make contributing to SRT-1 as easy and transparent as possible, whether it's:
- Reporting a bug
- Discussing the current state of the code
- Submitting a fix
- Proposing new features
- Becoming a maintainer

## Our Development Process
1. **Fork** the repository on GitHub.
2. **Clone** it down to your local machine.
3. **Commit** your changes to your own branch.
4. **Push** your work back up to your fork.
5. Submit a **Pull Request** so that we can review your changes.

## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)
**How do we ensure your contributions protect the project?**

By contributing to SRT-1 Core, you continuously agree that your contributions are licensed under the Apache 2.0 License. 

To merge your Pull Request into the canonical `SeedClassIntelligence/SRT1-CORE` repository, we require you to sign our **Contributor License Agreement (CLA)**. 
- When you open your first Pull Request, a GitHub Action bot will automatically comment on your PR asking you to sign the CLA.
- Signing the CLA grants Seed Class Intelligence the right to license and distribute your upstreamed code as part of the core product, while you retain the original copyright to your specific contribution.
- We cannot review or merge your code until this agreement is signed.

## Any Contributions You Make Will Be Under the Apache 2.0 License
In short, when you submit code changes, your submissions are understood to be under the same [Apache 2.0 License](LICENSE) that covers the project. Feel free to contact the maintainers if that's a concern.

## Forks and Downstream Customization
You are officially allowed by the Apache 2.0 license to fork this repository and modify it for your internal team or personal usage without sending changes back to us. However, if you want your modifications integrated into this canonical codebase, you must follow the Pull Request and CLA process outlined above.

Thank you for helping us trace and protect our codebase intelligence!
